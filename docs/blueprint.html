02 · How it's built

One website, one console

The public website and the team's editing console live in one project. Editors change content in the console; the website shows it straight away. Videos stay on YouTube / Instagram / Facebook — the site links and embeds them.

Who comes in

🪪 Party membersSign in with Member ID → member portal
🛡️ TeamLeadership & editors with role emails + 2-step code
📰 MediaNewsroom, statements, press kit

Website (Next.js)All public pages, member portal, three languages. Hosted on Vercel.
⇅

⇅
🗄️ Database (PostgreSQL)Events, petitions, posts, groups, leaders, page text

▶️ Social platformsYouTube, Instagram, Facebook, X embeds · WhatsApp sharing
🛡️ CloudflareProtection against attacks and spam bots

The building blocks, in plain words

PieceWhat it doesWhy this choice
Next.js (React)Builds every page. Pages are pre-built so they load fast on slow mobile data and show proper previews when shared on WhatsApp.Fast, good for Google search & link previews; the team already knows React.
Payload CMS recommendedThe editing console. Gives ready-made forms, image upload, lists, search, drafts, history and per-role permissions.Runs inside the same project; roles like "edit only your own posts" are built in.

04 · Who can do what

Nine access levels

Each person sees and changes only what their role allows. Team logins use role emails (president@, media@, feedback@ …) on the party's own domain, so when an office-holder changes, the account and its history stay with the office.

#RoleHow they sign inWhat they can do
1VisitorNo loginRead everything public; send feedback; ask a group for help
2SupporterGoogle or email codeSign petitions, request a petition, RSVP to events, track their own requests
3Member anonymousMember ID + password (invite only)Member portal: printables, propose petitions/campaigns/events (reviewed), submit ground reports (verified)
4Content EditorTeam loginCreate stories, blogs, reels, explainers — edit or delete only their own. No landing page, no statements
5Language EditorTeam loginFix Bangla / Hindi on any page, Wikipedia-style; every edit logged and reversible
6Admin EditorTeam login + 2-stepLanding page (hero, ticker, featured, numbers), everyone's posts, events, groups, leaders; verify members' reports; manage editors
7LeadershipRole email + 2-stepOnly role that issues Official Statements. Approve petitions & campaigns; open the member vault
8State PresidentRole email + 2-stepEverything Leadership can + give or remove any role
9Tech AdminSeparate account + 2-stepSettings, backups, fixes — no political powers, no member data

Rules that never bend

🔒 Members stay anonymous

Publicly and to other members, a member is only "Member · district". Names, phones and addresses are seen by the State President and Leadership only.

🟥 Statements = Leadership

Each official statement gets an ID (e.g. WBPCC/ST/2026/041) and a QR code, so a forwarded message can be checked.

📜 Everything is logged

Every change by the team is recorded — who, what, when, before & after — and can be undone.

07 · Translations & private data

Two kinds of data, kept apart

🌍 Public content

Events, petitions (text & counts), posts, groups, leaders, page text. Anyone can read it. Stored with English, Bangla and Hindi side by side.

🔒 Private records

Membership applications, members' contact details, formal-petition signer names & addresses, feedback and help tickets. Kept in a separate locked store, readable only by the roles that need it; every opening is logged.

Who sees private records

RecordVisible toShown publicly
Membership application / member detailsState President, LeadershipNothing — only "Member · district" on their reports
Online petition signaturePetition owner, LeadershipCount; first name + district only if the signer ticks the box
Formal petition (for government)Leadership; the authority it's submitted toCount only
Feedback / complaint / help ticketThe inbox it was routed to (e.g. complaints@)Nothing
"Near me" locationNobody — used only inside the visitor's own browserNothing

→

→

Legal pages (privacy, terms) are translated by a person after a lawyer approves them — never by machine.

Legal basis

The site is designed around India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023: a clear privacy notice, consent before collecting, data used only for the stated purpose, and the right to see, correct or erase it. The privacy policy and terms on the site are drafts for a lawyer to finalise. Online signatures are a record of support, not legal digital signatures under the IT Act.

08 · Instagram, YouTube & other channels (2/2)

Posting a reel — step by step

1
Post on Instagram as usual from the party's account (or YouTube / Facebook).
2
Copy the post link (Share → Copy link).
3
Console → Newsroom → New post. Choose Reel, Video or Blog and paste the link. The thumbnail and platform are picked up from the link.
4
Write the headline and the story in English. Pick the type — story, verified ground report or (Leadership only) official statement — and the district.
5
Auto-translate to Bangla and Hindi with one button; a Language Editor can polish it later.
6
Publish. It appears on the homepage rail and the Newsroom; sharing the page on WhatsApp shows a proper preview.

Golden rule for automation

Anything pulled in automatically from YouTube or Instagram lands as a draft, never straight on the site. An editor adds the written story, picks the type and district, and publishes. This keeps the Newsroom "Verified. Official. Real." — a hacked or mistaken social post can't appear on the website by itself.

Accounts to prepare

Needed for Phase 1

  • The party's official handles (e.g. @INCWestBengal) on each platform
  • A WhatsApp Channel link
  • The website domain (wbpcc.org or incwb.org — to confirm)
  • Role email accounts on that domain (president@, media@, feedback@ …)

Only if we automate later

  • Instagram switched to a Professional account, linked to the Facebook Page
  • A Meta developer app (free) owned by a party account
  • A YouTube API key (free quota)
  • WhatsApp Business Platform — paid, a later decision
Platform rules change often. Exact API steps, permissions and costs will be re-checked when we build the backend.

09 · Security, roadmap & decisions

What's next

Security built in

🛡️ Front door

Cloudflare in front of the site; spam protection on every form; limits on repeated attempts.

🔑 Logins

2-step codes for every team account; invite-only membership; no passwords stored by us for Google sign-ins.

💾 Backups

Daily database backups; the audit log can't be edited by editors.
